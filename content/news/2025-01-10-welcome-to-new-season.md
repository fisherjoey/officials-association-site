# Welcome back, officials!

The Basketball Officials Association is thrilled to announce the start of the 2024-2025 basketball season, with increased participation across all levels of play.

## Season highlights

This year brings several developments: an expanded training program with new modules on game management and advanced positioning, new digital tools for assignment management and communication, and a mentorship initiative pairing experienced officials with newcomers.

## Important dates

- **January 15**: Season kickoff meeting (virtual)
- **January 22-23**: Referee clinic for all levels
- **February 5**: Rule interpretation workshop
- **Monthly**: Ongoing development sessions throughout the season

## Get involved

Whether you're a seasoned veteran or new to officiating, there are plenty of ways to get involved. Attend training sessions to sharpen your skills, join the mentorship program to share your expertise or learn from experienced officials, and represent us at local basketball events.

## Looking ahead

We're committed to maintaining the highest standards of officiating. Your dedication and professionalism make this possible, and together we'll keep the game fair while helping basketball grow in our community.

Stay tuned for more updates throughout the season, and don't hesitate to reach out to the executive team with any questions or suggestions.

Here's to a great season ahead!
